Joint Venture FAQ

Who can apply for joint venture?

Interior contractors, designers, business owners, modular kitchen businesses, furniture companies, interior studios, and growing interior companies with proven experience.

How does the partnership work?

After mutual discussion and evaluation, we create a customized partnership agreement that outlines revenue sharing, responsibilities, project allocation, and support mechanisms.

What type of businesses can collaborate?

Any interior-related business including contractors, designers, studios, kitchen specialists, furniture companies, and established interior service providers.

What are the financial terms?

Financial terms are customized based on business evaluation, project type, and mutual agreement. Contact us for a detailed discussion.

How can I discuss this opportunity?

Submit your business details through our enquiry form or contact us directly. Our partnership team will reach out within 48 hours to schedule a discussion.

Is training provided to partner teams?

Yes, we provide training on our methodologies, standards, and best practices to ensure seamless collaboration and high-quality project delivery.
