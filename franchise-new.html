Franchise FAQ

Who can apply for franchise?

Entrepreneurs with business acumen, passion for interior design training, and entrepreneurial spirit can apply. Educational background in interior design is not mandatory.

What is included in the franchise package?

Each package includes office setup, teaching materials, brand support, training for trainers, business guidance, and marketing support based on the package tier.

Is training provided to franchisees?

Yes, comprehensive training is provided to franchisees on teaching methodology, business operations, client management, and marketing strategies.

Is ongoing business support provided?

Yes, Interior Gurukulam provides continuous support including regular mentoring, updates on training materials, marketing assistance, and operational guidance.

What are the expected returns?

Returns depend on local market conditions, student enrollment, course pricing, and operational efficiency. Contact us for detailed financial projections.
